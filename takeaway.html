The "pass-through" story: Water Purchases (GLWA) and Sewer Charges (Oakland County) together total $23.7M — that's 55% of all fund spending. These are external costs the city doesn't control. When GLWA raises rates 5.8% and Oakland County raises rates 9.9%, it directly impacts resident bills.
