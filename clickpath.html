Toggle to Expenditure View — The Rate Story

12:00 – 16:00  •  Same fund, expense view
🖱
Click path: Toggle from Revenue to Expenditure view → Show bar chart
Say This

"On the expense side, the fund spends $43.5 million. The two largest line items tell the story:"

"Water Purchases — $14.2 million. This is what Rochester Hills pays the Great Lakes Water Authority for raw water. GLWA raised their charges 5.8% — the city doesn't control that cost."

"Sewer Charges — $9.5 million to Oakland County for sewer service. They raised rates 9.9%. Again, a pass-through cost."

"Together, those two are $23.7 million — 55% of total fund expenses. When a citizen asks 'why did my bill go up?' — the answer is right here, in two clicks."

Key Expenditures
  • Water Purchases (GLWA): $14.2M — 32.8% of fund
  • Sewer Charges (Oakland Co.): $9.5M — 21.9%
  • W&S Capital Transfers: $6.0M — 13.8%
  • Salaries & Wages: $3.7M — 8.5%
Anticipate This Question

"Why are rates going up 8.7%?" — GLWA +5.8%, Oakland County +9.9%, infrastructure investment, and maintaining 25% fund balance for operational stability. The city chose the "break-even option" — lowest rates possible while maintaining service.

"Why did my water bill go up?" — Navigate to expenditure view live. "The largest cost is water from GLWA — $14.2M. GLWA raised rates 5.8%, Oakland County raised sewer rates 9.9%. These are pass-through costs the city doesn't control."

Mystery Guest — Universal Response

Don't panic. Ask them to introduce themselves. If you don't know the answer: "That's a great question. I want to give you an accurate answer rather than speculate. Let me follow up with our team on that specific detail."
